[b]One key switches your colony between normal and alert status, and each status remembers how you set up your colonists.[/b]

There's nothing to set up and no menus to learn. Press the key, arrange your colonists the way you want them for a raid, and press it again to put everything back. Next time trouble comes, one press brings your alert setup straight back.

Colony Alert is manual, and it always will be. It never switches on by itself when a raid arrives. You decide when the colony goes on alert and when it stands down.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]One key:[/b] Press F9 to switch between normal and alert status. Press it again to switch back.
[*][b]Remembers both statuses:[/b] Whatever you change while on alert is kept for alert, and whatever you change in normal times is kept for normal.
[*][b]What switches:[/b] Each colonist's allowed area, schedule, work priorities, apparel, food, drug and reading policies, and hostility response, plus tame animals' allowed areas.
[*][b]No setup:[/b] The first time you go on alert, your colonists keep their normal settings until you change them.
[*][b]Always visible:[/b] A red border and an ALERT banner stay on screen while alert status is on. Click the banner to stand down.
[*][b]New arrivals:[/b] Colonists who join during an alert keep the settings they arrived with as their normal ones.
[*][b]Nothing breaks:[/b] If you delete an area or policy, colonists simply keep their current one for that setting.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Key:[/b] Change it in Options, Controls, Keyboard Configuration, under "In-game global". The default is F9.
[*][b]What switches:[/b] Turn each kind of setting on or off, including animals' allowed areas.
[*][b]Display:[/b] Turn the red border, the switching sound and the switching message on or off.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Mods that also switch these settings, such as Better Pawn Control's presets, will overwrite each other's changes, so use one or the other for the same settings.
Safe to add to an existing save. Stand down from alert before removing the mod, or your colonists keep their alert settings.
